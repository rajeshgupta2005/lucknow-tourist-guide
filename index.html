THE CITY OF NAWABS

A city with stories at every turn

Lucknow blends the elegance of its Nawabi past with lively markets, welcoming neighbourhoods and modern public spaces. This guide groups nearby attractions into a relaxed three-day visit.

🏛️

Heritage

Imambaras, historic gateways, clock towers and sites connected with 1857.

🧵

Culture & craft

Chikankari embroidery, traditional markets and Lucknow's famous tehzeeb.

🌳

Open spaces

Landscaped parks, gardens and relaxing views along the Gomti River.

FLAVOURS & FINDS

Taste and take home Lucknow

🍽️ Awadhi flavours

Try local specialities such as Awadhi biryani, kebabs and traditional breads. Ask for vegetarian options where preferred.

🍧 Street food & sweets

Explore chaat, kulfi and classic sweets from established local shops. Choose clean, busy food outlets.

🪡 Chikankari shopping

Look for hand-embroidered garments. Compare fabric, workmanship and prices before buying.

🎁 Souvenir ideas

Chikankari textiles, small handicrafts and locally made sweets packed suitably for travel.

TRAVEL SMART

Practical travel tips

Best season

October to March is generally more comfortable for sightseeing.

Getting around

Use a cab, auto or public transport. Group nearby Old City attractions to reduce backtracking.

Before visiting

Confirm opening hours, weekly closures, ticket prices and access rules from official sources.

Stay comfortable

Wear walking shoes, carry water and sun protection, and allow extra time for traffic.

Respect local places

Follow photography rules and local customs at religious and heritage sites.

Keep plans flexible

Suggested timings are approximate; adjust for weather, queues and your interests.
